The House

Before you go in

This is a House of endless halls and staircases, with the sea beneath it and cloud above, and statues on every wall. You are alone in it, and it is mostly sound.

  • Look and listen. Read the description, press listen, or type it.
  • Move. Press a door button under a description, or type go north. To go far, type travel to the Ninth Vestibule or tap a place on the Map.
  • Let it carry you. Press Wander and it walks on and on. Turn on Read descriptions aloud in Settings to be read to.
  • Keep a journal. Press Journal and write whatever you like. It is yours alone, and your journey, journal included, is here when you come back.
  • Share a place. The address always names the room you are in. Share sends it to someone.
  • Keys (when you are not typing): / to type, m map, w wander, j journal, r say it again, s sound, ? help.

Piranesi’s House

An endless building of halls and staircases, with the sea beneath it and the clouds above, and on every wall more silent statues than anyone could count. It is always the same house. The tides keep their own time.

You will arrive alone. Listen as much as you look.

Headphones are best. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Tap a room for its name. Drag to move the map.

Gold ring: you, and the way you face. Solid: halls you have been in. Dashed: seen through a door, not entered. Blue: water there now. Dashed line: a long passage. The map only remembers this visit.

    Install on an iPhone or iPad

    Installed, the House opens full screen from your home screen like an app, and keeps working with no connection.

    1. Open this page in Safari. (On iOS 16.4 or later, other browsers offer “Add to Home Screen” in their share menu too, but Safari is the most dependable.)
    2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (at the top on an iPad).
    3. Scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen. If you do not see it, scroll right to the bottom and tap Edit Actions, or check you are not in a private window.
    4. Keep or change the name, then tap Add. The House’s icon appears on your home screen.
    5. Open it once while you have a connection. That is when it stores everything it needs. After that it works in airplane mode.

    Your journey does not follow you in. An app on the home screen keeps its own storage, apart from Safari’s, so it starts with an empty journey. To bring yours in, use Take my journey with me in Settings: save a copy in Safari, then bring it into the installed app.

    Take my journey with me

    Your journey is where you have been, what you have found and marked, the way you remember, and your journal. It is kept in this browser only. To go on in another browser or on another device, save a copy here and bring it there. Nothing is sent anywhere: the copy is a file (or a block of text) that you carry yourself, and anyone who has it can read your journal, so keep it as you would a notebook.

    Begin a new journey?

    You will start again at the First Vestibule, knowing nothing: no memory of where you have been, no marks, nothing found, and no way back to remember. This cannot be undone, unless you have saved a copy.

    About, credits and privacy

    An unofficial fan project. Perhaps unlike you, I did not enjoy Susanna Clarke’s novel Piranesi. Her ideas on religion were far too aligned with the teachings of Satan. However I did love the idea of an infinitely explorable house in the novel. So I set out to create a faithful replica you can explore without immersion in Satanic teachings. It contains none of the book’s text. It is not endorsed by the author or the publisher. It is my hope that you enjoy exploring and getting lost.

    The place is also named for the eighteenth-century etcher Giovanni Battista Piranesi, whose drawings of enormous, impossible buildings inspired the halls' strange architecture. The House's statues, halls, tides and weather are generated from a seed, so everyone who uses the same seed finds the same House.

    What this page keeps, and what it sends

    • It sends nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. After the page has loaded, it needs no network at all.
    • On your device only, it keeps your settings and your journey: where you are, where you have been, what you have found and marked, the way you remember, and your journal, along with the files needed to open offline. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.
    • Taking it elsewhere is up to you: Settings, “Take my journey with me”, saves a copy as a file or a block of text that you carry yourself. Whoever has that copy can read your journal.
    • A shared link contains only the name of a room (and the level and world if they are unusual). Nobody else can see your marks, journal or settings.
    • Reading aloud uses your browser's own voices. Some browsers and devices use voices that run online, in which case the words being read are sent to that voice service by your browser, not by this page. Choose an offline voice in Settings if that matters to you.
