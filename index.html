The House

An endless building of halls and staircases, with the sea beneath it and the clouds above, and on every wall more silent statues than anyone could count. It is always the same house. The tides keep their own time.

You will arrive alone. Listen as much as you look: the House is mostly sound.

Headphones are best. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

An unofficial fan project inspired by a novel. It contains none of the book's text. Source on GitHub.

Settings

Everything starts on. Turn off what you do not want.

Changing the seed, the dead or the speed of time starts you again at the First Vestibule.
