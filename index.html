The House

Before you go in

This is a House of endless halls and staircases, with the sea beneath it and cloud above, and statues on every wall. You are alone in it, and it is mostly sound.

  • Look and listen. Read the description, press listen, or type it.
  • Move. Press a door button under a description, or type go north. To go far, type travel to the Ninth Vestibule or tap a place on the Map.
  • Let it carry you. Press Wander and it walks on and on. Turn on Read descriptions aloud in Settings to be read to.
  • Share a place. The address always names the room you are in. Share sends it to someone.
  • Keys (when you are not typing): / to type, m map, w wander, r say it again, s sound, ? help.

Piranesi’s House

An endless building of halls and staircases, with the sea beneath it and the clouds above, and on every wall more silent statues than anyone could count. It is always the same house. The tides keep their own time.

You will arrive alone. Listen as much as you look.

Headphones are best. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

An unofficial fan project inspired by Piranesi, a novel. It contains none of the book's text. Source on GitHub.

Tap a room for its name. Drag to move the map.

Gold ring: you, and the way you face. Solid: halls you have been in. Dashed: seen through a door, not entered. Blue: water there now. Dashed line: a long passage. The map only remembers this visit.

About, credits and privacy

An unofficial fan project, made out of admiration for Piranesi, the novel by Susanna Clarke. It contains none of the book's text; everything said here is written for this project. It is not endorsed by the author or the publisher.

The place is also named for the eighteenth-century etcher Giovanni Battista Piranesi, whose drawings of enormous, impossible buildings inspired the halls' strange architecture. The House's statues, halls, tides and weather are generated from a seed, so everyone who uses the same seed finds the same House.

What this page keeps, and what it sends

  • It sends nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. After the page has loaded, it needs no network at all.
  • On your device only, it keeps your settings, what you have noticed that earlier visitors left (the found list) and the places you mark, and the files needed to open offline. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.
  • A shared link contains only the name of a room (and the level and world if they are unusual). Nobody else can see your marks or settings.
  • Reading aloud uses your browser's own voices. Some browsers and devices use voices that run online, in which case the words being read are sent to that voice service by your browser, not by this page. Choose an offline voice in Settings if that matters to you.
